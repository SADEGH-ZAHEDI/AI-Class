Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a background in finance?

No. You need the willingness to learn and the discipline to follow a process. Training starts from the fundamentals, and in Phase 1 you practise on a demo account before any real money is involved.

What is a demo account?

A demo account is a practice trading account that uses virtual funds under real market conditions. You learn how markets behave and build your skills without putting your family's capital at risk.

When do I move to a real account?

When you have completed the training and shown consistent, disciplined results on the demo account. We decide together. It depends on your readiness, not on a fixed date.

How do leverage and profit sharing work in Phase 3?

Once you have built a consistent track record on your real account, you may be offered additional capital to manage alongside your family portfolio, within strict risk limits, and earn a share of the profits it generates. Eligibility and terms are agreed individually and depend on applicable regulation. Leverage increases risk as well as potential return.

Why only one person from each family?

One clear steward means clear responsibility and consistent decisions. It also lets us give each person real time for training and support. The rest of the family keeps full visibility of the portfolio.

Who holds the assets?

Your family's assets stay in your family's own name. You have permanent, transparent access to your portfolio at all times.

Do you guarantee returns?

No. No honest advisor can. Markets go up and down, and you may lose some or all of the capital invested. What we offer is a disciplined process, transparent reporting and close support, with a focus on protecting your principal.
